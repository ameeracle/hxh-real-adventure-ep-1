# Hunter x Hunter: The Real Adventure - Episode 1

## Short description

A fan-made, LEGO-style action adventure that retells the first chapters of Hunter x Hunter: from Gon's encounter with Kite to the road to the Hunter Exam, with brick-built worlds, comic cutscenes and puzzles drawn straight from the manga.

## Long description

Gon Freecss has one dream: to become a Hunter and find the father who left him behind. *Hunter x Hunter: The Real Adventure* is a fan-made tribute that follows his journey from the forests of Whale Island to the doors of the Hunter Exam, rebuilt brick by brick in a toy-like 3D world.

Episode 1 adapts the opening chapters of Yoshihiro Togashi's manga page by page. Face a giant foxbear and meet Kite and his Crazy Slots clown. Track Kon through the forest and land the legendary Master of the Swamp to win Mito's permission. Then set sail: answer the seacranes from the masthead, patch a flooding hold, care for seasick passengers, and leap into the storm to save a sailor while Kurapika and Leorio hold your legs. At Dolle, find the hidden townsfolk and survive the old woman's impossible two-choice quiz. Under the lone pine, three friends face three different tests: Gon chases a shapeshifting Kiriko, Kurapika uncovers a lie hidden in ancient tattoos, and Leorio treats an "injured husband" without ever guessing who he really is.

Features:

- **A story told like a toy adventure:** short, funny cutscenes with expressive brick characters, reaction marks and slapstick, written from the manga's own dialogue.
- **Play as the whole team:** switch between Gon, Kurapika, Leorio and Killua, each with his own weapon and skills. The story hands control to whoever a task needs.
- **Puzzles from the source:** read the seacranes' warning, steer through giant swells, match a tattoo to the right record, pick the right tool from Leorio's briefcase. Every challenge comes from what happens in the chapter.
- **Living worlds:** Whale Island, a three-masted ship in a night storm, the crooked town of Dolle and the forest of the Navigators, with day, dusk, rain and lightning.
- **Optional mini-games:** talk with the seacranes in Birdtalk, or find the one clean can in Tonpa's shuffle.
- **Play your way:** keyboard and mouse or controller on Windows, and on-screen touch controls on Android.

Available for Windows and Android.
